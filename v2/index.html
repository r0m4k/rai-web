RAI · GOVERNANCE & POLICY

A speaker series with a focus on AI governance that aims to make the rules for AI a public conversation.

Every other Thursday during term, researchers, lawyers, policymakers and practitioners present one clear argument about how AI should be built, audited and held to account, followed by open questions from the room. Free and open to all, in ISEC 102 and online.
